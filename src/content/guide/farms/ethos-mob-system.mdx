**Good:** compact, quick and cheap to build, and it doesn't cause lag, because the water never changes once it's placed and nothing moves. **Not so good:** nothing pushes mobs off the pads, so they have to walk off on their own, and some get stuck.

## How it works

Each layer has **12 pads**, each 3 × 3 and 2 blocks tall, standing in water channels. Mobs spawn on the **8 edge blocks** of each pad. Four more spawning spaces sit on blocks placed over the corner water sources: **100 spaces** per layer.

A mob walks off a pad, drops 2 blocks into the water, and the current carries it to the **hole in the middle**, where it falls through the centre of every layer below. Water from the end of each arm flows 7 blocks and stops one block short of the hole, so you don't need signs to hold it back.

- **No spiders.** A spider is 1.4 blocks wide and spawns centred on a block, so it needs the whole 3 × 3 area around that block to be clear. The stone slab in the middle of each pad is inside that area for all 8 edge blocks, so spiders can't spawn anywhere on the pad.
- **No endermen.** The next layer's floor sits right on top of the walls, so each pad has 2 blocks of headroom. Endermen need 3.

## Building it

Etho builds each layer in this order:

1. **Lay the floor.** Pick the centre, leave that one block open, and spread the floor out in the shape of the bottom map.
2. **Place the bottom block of all 12 pads.** It shows you how far the floor needs to reach.
3. **Build up the walls,** leaving the 8 water spots open: the end of each arm and each corner of the ring.
4. **On the second block up, cover each ring-corner water spot** with a block. These are the four bonus spawning spaces.
5. **Finish the walls** to 4 blocks above the floor.
6. **Add the top block of each pad last,** so you can still jump around the layer while you build.
7. **Place the water.** Etho suggests bringing ice instead of buckets. It stacks, and breaking it leaves a water source.
8. **Put a stone slab on the middle of each pad.**
9. **Start the next layer's floor** straight on top of the walls.

## Stacking and killing

Etho recommends **5 to 10 layers**. In his test world, where nothing else could spawn, 5 layers reached the hostile cap of about 79 within a minute or so. In a normal world, caves you missed take part of the cap, so more layers help. More layers also spread the mobs out, so they jam less in the middle.

Past 10 layers, your time is better spent lighting caves. The whole farm has a few hundred spawning spaces, and the caves around you have thousands. See [the mob cap](/mechanics/mob-spawning/#the-mob-cap).

Build the bottom layer **at least 24 blocks above the ground**, so the fall through the middle kills the mobs. Etho calls it the quickest way to get lots of drops. Mobs killed by a fall don't drop experience, though. If you want XP, make the drop 22 blocks so they land on one hit point, and finish them yourself (see [fall damage](/mechanics/fall-damage/)).

Five layers plus a cover on top is 26 blocks tall.

## Letting endermen in

Add one more block to the walls before you start each new layer, making layers 6 blocks high. That gives the pads 3 blocks of headroom. Build from something endermen can't pick up, so not dirt, grass or sand.

## Differences in 1.1

### The lamp switch

<AppliesTo versions={['1.2', '1.3', '1.4+']} note="Redstone lamps arrived in 1.2." />

In the video, Etho puts 8 redstone lamps in each layer's ceiling so he can switch the farm off. Lamps aren't in 1.1. To stop it spawning, put a torch on each pad.

### Mobs getting stuck on pads

Etho says that a few updates before the video, mobs gained an idle timer: a mob more than 32 blocks from a player can go to sleep and stop moving. Mobs asleep on a pad stay there until they despawn, which is this design's main weakness.

<Unconfirmed basis="EthosLab says the idle timer was added 'a few patches' before August 2012">
  Whether 1.1 has the idle timer yet. If it doesn't, mobs never go to sleep on the pads and the farm should
  run better in 1.1 than in the video. If it does, stand where the pads you care about are within 32 blocks.
</Unconfirmed>
